# Hagz — Mascot Guidelines

## 1. Character Concept

The Hagz mascot is a custom geometric character internally referred to as:

**Dash**

Dash represents the energetic and fast-moving personality of Hagz.

The character is designed to become a recognizable brand face rather than a decorative illustration.

---

## 2. Character Personality

Dash is:

* Energetic.
* Fast.
* Confident.
* Competitive.
* Friendly.
* Social.
* Organized.

Dash represents the feeling of:

> "Never let a game fall through."

---

## 3. Visual Design

Dash uses:

* A forward-leaning silhouette.
* Aerodynamic geometric forms.
* Intersecting planes.
* Deep Navy as the primary body color.
* Lime as the signature accent.
* White and Light Gray for contrast.

The signature visor frame uses the same geometric language as the Hagz H monogram.

---

## 4. Character Style

Dash should use a:

* Flat vector style.
* Clean geometric construction.
* Minimal visual detail.
* Strong silhouette.
* Friendly expression.

Avoid:

* Realistic rendering.
* 3D appearance.
* Heavy shadows.
* Gradients.
* Excessive glowing effects.
* Generic robot appearance.

---

## 5. Character Role

Dash can appear in:

* Onboarding.
* Booking confirmation.
* Empty states.
* Error states.
* Fully booked states.
* Loading states.
* Notifications.
* Social media.
* Promotional materials.
* Stickers.

---

## 6. Character Principle

Dash should communicate personality through:

* Pose.
* Expression.
* Movement.
* Context.

The character should not depend on large amounts of text to communicate its role.
