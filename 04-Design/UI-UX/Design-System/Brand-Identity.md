# Hagz — Brand Identity

## 1. Brand Name

**Hagz**

The name represents the core action of the platform: booking a sports court.

---

## 2. Brand Personality

Hagz should feel:

* Modern.
* Confident.
* Energetic.
* Friendly.
* Fast.
* Organized.

The brand should feel like a platform built by people who understand how players actually think about finding and booking a place to play.

---

## 3. Brand Character

Hagz is not designed as a traditional sports brand.

Instead, the brand combines:

**Technology + Sport + Simplicity**

The visual language should communicate movement and energy while maintaining a professional digital-product appearance.

---

## 4. Brand Voice

Hagz communication should be:

* Short.
* Clear.
* Confident.
* Friendly.
* Direct.

Examples:

**Preferred:**

> Find your court.

> Pick your time.

> Game on.

> Your court is booked.

**Avoid:**

> Don't miss this amazing opportunity!!!

> Best football courts at unbelievable prices!!!

The brand should avoid aggressive sales language.

---

## 5. Core Brand Idea

### Less Searching. More Playing.

Hagz reduces the effort required to find and book a sports court so users can focus on the actual game.
