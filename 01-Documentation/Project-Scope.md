# Hagz — Project Scope

## 1. Scope Statement

The scope of the first version of Hagz is to develop a web-based platform that enables players to discover and book sports courts while allowing court owners to manage their courts and booking requests.

An administrator will manage platform-level operations and approve newly submitted courts.

## 2. In Scope

### Player

* Account registration.
* Authentication.
* Court search.
* Filtering by area.
* Filtering by price.
* Viewing court details.
* Viewing available time slots.
* Creating a booking.
* Cancelling a booking.
* Viewing bookings.

### Court Owner

* Account registration.
* Authentication.
* Adding courts.
* Managing court information.
* Uploading court photos.
* Setting court prices.
* Defining available time slots.
* Viewing incoming bookings.
* Accepting bookings.
* Rejecting bookings.

### Administrator

* Authentication.
* Viewing newly submitted courts.
* Approving courts.
* Rejecting courts.
* Managing users.

## 3. Out of Scope

The following features are outside the scope of the initial version:

* Online payment processing.
* Wallet systems.
* Subscription plans.
* Advertising system.
* Advanced recommendation systems.
* Loyalty and reward systems.
* Real-time navigation.
* Complex analytics and business intelligence.
* Multi-platform native mobile applications.

These features may be considered in future versions.

## 4. Scope Constraints

The project will initially focus on the core booking workflow.

The implementation should prioritize:

1. Correctness.
2. Clear user flows.
3. Secure authentication and authorization.
4. Reliable booking management.
5. Maintainable architecture.
6. Usable interface.

## 5. Future Expansion

The system can later be extended with:

* Online payments.
* Notifications.
* Reviews and ratings.
* Favorite courts.
* Advanced search.
* Map integration.
* Mobile applications.
* Court performance analytics.
* Promotional offers.
